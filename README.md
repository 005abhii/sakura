# Sakura
A Complete Teacher Lifecycle Management System
 Empowering Educators. Elevating Education.

 
In today’s dynamic and increasingly globalized educational landscape, empowering teachers throughout their professional journey is key to achieving consistent, high-quality student outcomes. Sakura — named after the Japanese cherry blossom symbolizing renewal and growth — is a comprehensive Teacher Lifecycle Management Platform that enables international schools to support their educators across every stage: from onboarding to development, performance management to well-being, and recognition to retention.
Designed with a deep understanding of modern school challenges — including burnout, compliance, inclusion, and retention — Sakura acts as a single, intelligent hub for teacher enablement, engagement, and excellence.

The Challenges Sakura Solves:
Disconnected systems for workload, performance, and development tracking


Rising stress, burnout, and work-life imbalance among educators


Inconsistent recognition and retention strategies


Privacy and compliance concerns, especially under GDPR


Difficulty forecasting future staffing needs


Limited support for self-reflection and pedagogical improvement
